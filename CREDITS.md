# Credits

## The sky is drawn, not copied

Arcade Scroller Universe draws every star, nebula and galaxy itself. It contains
no photographs or other images of the sky, neither from the sources below nor
from anyone else. The invented galaxy types are inspired by NASA/ESA images;
none of those images is included.

The real sky comes from a small catalogue of facts: positions, sizes,
brightness, orientation and names of 261 deep-sky objects and 223 stars, and 22
constellation figures. Most values were compiled from the references below; the
position angles of the Messier galaxies, the morphology types, the feature tags
and some magnitudes are our own rough estimates. All of it is rough on purpose. **The MIT License covers the program code. It claims no
rights to these facts; they remain with their sources.**

## Star map

The 22 constellation stick figures follow, in simplified form, the figures of
the IAU constellation charts by the IAU and Sky & Telescope magazine (Roger
Sinnott & Rick Fienberg), <https://astro4edu.org/iau-constellation-diagrams/>,
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The
program stores them as chains of star ids and draws them itself; no chart is
included.

## Deep fields

The Hubble Deep Field and the Hubble Ultra Deep Field (Hubble Space Telescope,
NASA and ESA) and Webb's First Deep Field, SMACS 0723 (James Webb Space
Telescope, NASA, ESA and CSA), appear by name, position and size only.
